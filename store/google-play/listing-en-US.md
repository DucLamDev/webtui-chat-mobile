### Full description

WebTUI Chat is a communication app for teams that use a WebTUI Chat workspace.
Sign in to a server provided by your organization, then keep direct messages,
channels, files, calls, and notifications in one place.

Use WebTUI Chat to:

- Message teammates in direct conversations and channels.
- Share images, documents, voice notes, and other files you choose.
- Start voice and video calls when your workspace enables calling.
- Receive message and call notifications on supported servers.
- Search conversations, pin important content, and review recent files.
- Manage contacts and friend requests inside your workspace.
- Block users, report messages or accounts, and manage privacy settings.
- Review sessions and request account deletion from the app.

WebTUI Chat is designed for self-hosted and organization-managed deployments.
Conversation data is sent to the WebTUI Chat server selected by you or your
organization. The production app uses HTTPS/WSS for server connections and
stores session data in the secure storage provided by the operating system.

Some features, including push notifications, SSO, files, bots, and calls,
depend on the configuration of the server you connect to. You need an active
account and server address from your organization or service provider to use the
app.
